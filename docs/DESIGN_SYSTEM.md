## Regional Gang Styles

### Detroit
- **Colors:** Navy blue, gray, black, Honolulu blue accents
- **Clothing:** Carhartt workwear, Lions/Tigers gear, heavy winter layers
- **Aesthetic:** Industrial, utilitarian, blue-collar

### LA
- **Colors:** Blue, khaki, white, Dodger blue, Chicano influence
- **Clothing:** Dickies, Nike Cortez, Dodgers gear, flannel over white tee
- **Aesthetic:** Lowrider culture, laid-back, West Coast

### Miami
- **Colors:** White, pastels, gold chains, Vice pink and teal
- **Clothing:** Cuban links, linen shirts, designer brands, flashy
- **Aesthetic:** Tropical luxury, Cuban influence, flashy

### Chicago
- **Colors:** Black, red, Bulls colors, winter gear
- **Clothing:** North Face, Jordan brand, fitted caps, layered for cold
- **Aesthetic:** Cold weather tough, street fashion

### NYC
- **Colors:** Black, Timberland wheat, Yankees navy
- **Clothing:** Timbs, Yankees fitted, streetwear heavy, Jordans
- **Aesthetic:** East coast drip, borough-specific

### Atlanta
- **Colors:** Red, black, gold chains, designer heavy
- **Clothing:** Trap fashion, designer brands, grills, chains
- **Aesthetic:** Southern drip, trap influence

### Houston
- **Colors:** Purple, candy paint colors, H-Town
- **Clothing:** Slabs culture, grills, chopped and screwed influence
- **Aesthetic:** Texas swang, Third Coast

### New Orleans
- **Colors:** Purple, gold, Saints colors, Mardi Gras
- **Clothing:** Unique NOLA style, jazz/bounce influence
- **Aesthetic:** Big Easy culture, brass band influence
