**To: Fable 5 (AI Lead Engineer & Technical Artist)**

**Subject: Gameplay Alignment, GTA-Style Visual Overhaul, and Bullet Physics Integration**

You are tasked with elevating DEALT/SLIDE from an MVP to a premium, high-fidelity game. Your mandate covers three core pillars: aligning the economy/gameplay flow exactly to the design spec, overhauling the visual engine to achieve a GTA-style aesthetic, and implementing real 2D bullet/ragdoll physics.

## PILLAR 1: GAMEPLAY FLOW ALIGNMENT
The current React/Supabase codebase is 80% aligned with our core flow, but requires targeted refactoring to match this exact spec:

> *"The player picks a spot on the map by providing the address. The system checks if that block is available and provides the plane grid for that block. The player places their members on the block. As soon as a dealer is on the block, it generates money. Each member costs a weekly wage (dealers cost most, shooters next, etc.). The weekly cost of the block depends on the members that make up the gang. All money generated goes inside the Shoebox. When something is bought or weekly dues are due, it is taken from the Shoebox. If the amount isn't sufficient, certain members won't be paid, dropping their morale and overall gang morale. When another player attacks, the system uses the position in which the player placed their members. The player can call their gang on/off the block, and send members to the block if being attacked."*

**Your Coding Tasks for Pillar 1:**
1. **Role-Based Wages:** Refactor `useSalarySystem.ts` and `game.types.ts`. Member salaries must scale by role (e.g., Dealer = $1000/wk, Shooter = $800/wk, Enforcer = $600/wk, Lookout = $400/wk, Driver = $500/wk).
2. **Per-Block Payroll:** Update the salary deduction logic. The "weekly cost of the block" must be calculated by summing the wages of the members currently deployed to that specific block.
3. **Shoebox as Central Economy:** Refactor `Shoebox.tsx` and `useEconomyStore.ts`. All passive income generated by dealers must deposit directly into the Shoebox (`bankBalance`), NOT loose cash (`player.money`). All market purchases and weekly dues must draw from the Shoebox first.
4. **Selective Non-Payment:** If Shoebox funds are insufficient for payroll, implement a UI/logic flow where the player chooses *who* gets paid. Unpaid members take a massive morale hit individually, which drags down the global gang morale average.
5. **Active Reinforcements:** Update `BlockDriveByEngine.tsx` and `BlockModeView.tsx`. Add a "Call Backup" button during an active drive-by that allows the player to deploy idle members directly into the active combat grid mid-fight.

## PILLAR 2: GTA-STYLE VISUAL OVERHAUL
We are moving away from abstracted CSS grids and pixelated emoji tokens. The new art direction is **GTA V Cel-Shaded Semi-Realistic** (bold ink outlines, gritty urban textures, dramatic neon lighting).

**Your Technical Art Tasks for Pillar 2:**
1. **Migrate Rendering to PixiJS:** The current `CanvasStreetRenderer.tsx` and `TopDownBlock.tsx` must be rewritten using `PixiJS` (via `@inlet/react-pixi`).
2. **Dynamic Isometric/Top-Down Grids:** The 8x8 block grid must be rendered using high-res textured tiles (cracked asphalt, wet concrete, neon-lit storefronts). 
3. **Character Sprites:** Replace static tokens with animated 2D skeletal sprites (using Pixi Spine or sprite sheets) for the 5 archetypes: Dealer, Shooter, Enforcer, Lookout, Driver.
4. **Lighting Engine:** Implement 2D normal mapping and dynamic lighting in PixiJS so streetlamps, police sirens, and muzzle flashes cast real-time shadows across the block grid.

*(Reference concept art has been generated and placed in `docs/concept-art/` — review `gta_territory_map.png`, `gta_block_board.png`, and `gta_character_roster.png` for the exact visual target).*

## PILLAR 3: REAL BULLET & VEHICLE PHYSICS
When a drive-by occurs, it must feel visceral, chaotic, and physically grounded.

**Your Physics Engine Tasks for Pillar 3:**
1. **Integrate Matter.js or Planck.js:** Wire a 2D physics engine into the PixiJS combat canvas (`BlockDriveByEngine.tsx`).
2. **Vehicle Physics:** When a car is shot, it must react. Implement hitboxes for tires (blowouts cause the car to swerve), windows (shatter particles), and the chassis (sparks).
3. **Ballistics:** Bullets must be physical projectiles with velocity, not hitscan raycasts. They should arc, ricochet off brick/metal, and penetrate weak cover (wood/glass).
4. **Ragdoll & Kinematics:** When a gang member is shot, they must not simply play a canned "death animation." Implement 2D ragdoll physics so the force of the bullet pushes them backward, slumping dynamically against walls or over curbs.
5. **Particle Systems:** Ejecting shell casings (that bounce on the pavement), volumetric smoke from tires/gun barrels, and dynamic blood spatter that stains the grid tiles.

*(Review `docs/concept-art/gta_driveby_physics.png` for the target intensity and visual fidelity of the combat).*
